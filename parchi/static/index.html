Product The name printed on the bill. Put the pack size in it (“Amul Butter 100 g”, “Milk 1 L”) and Parchi uses it to work out packs: 2 litre milk → 2 × 1 L.
Sold by kg / litre: priced per kg or litre, so 0.25 kg can be billed. piece / pack: priced per item, always whole numbers.
Price ₹ Per kg, litre, piece or pack. This is the only place prices come from; the AI never sets a price.
Size options For one product in several sizes: give them the same group (e.g. “maggi”) and mark them small or big. “Chhota/lahan” then picks small, “bada/motha” picks big; otherwise Parchi asks.
Customer words What customers actually type, separated by “;” e.g. ande; anda; अंडे. Corrections you make while billing are also remembered.
Stock You set this. Parchi doesn't count stock. Switch it off when you run out: orders for it turn red, it's left off the bill, and the reply says “not available right now”.
